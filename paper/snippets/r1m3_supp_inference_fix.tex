We ran \nChains{} chains of \nSamples{} samples each using the NUTS sampler. All parameters achieved $\hat{R} < 1.01$ (Table~\ref{tab:convergence}). Bulk effective sample sizes ranged from 2657 to 5453 and tail-ESS from 1334 to 3195. \texttt{N\_IL2\_CD8} had the lowest tail-ESS (1334); together with \texttt{N\_IL2\_CD4} (2549), it was one of two parameters below the 3000 threshold originally claimed. Both are division-destiny priors derived from weakly related proxy data (see the corresponding SubmodelTargets), with deliberately broad prior widths reflecting cross-stimulation and cross-species translation uncertainty, so their lower tail-ESS reflects diffuse identification rather than mixing failure. Sampling took about 42 seconds on an M-series MacBook Air.
