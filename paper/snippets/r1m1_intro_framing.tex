The LLM is reliable for forward-model choices and observable code when given sufficient context, but cannot judge when context is missing, and can independently miss inputs or lose track of constraints across long records. The schema and validators make the modeler's context supply (unit conventions, source-applicability decisions) explicit, and the modeler's review of every record catches the LLM lapses the validators do not detect, so the calibration record shows both what the LLM produced and what the modeler supplied or corrected.
