All parameters converged ($\hat{R} < 1.01$), with bulk-ESS $> 2600$ and tail-ESS $> 1300$ throughout (Table~\ref{tab:convergence}). Recognizable quantities fall in clinically measured ranges: the inferred tumor doubling time of $\sim$130 days is consistent with PDAC volume-doubling-time measurements \citep{ahnTimeProgressionPancreatic2017, jangMissedPancreaticDuctal2015}. Not all estimates are equally constrained: Supplementary Section~\ref{sec:inference-results} notes that the IL-2-dependent CD8+ division count falls below its expected range.
