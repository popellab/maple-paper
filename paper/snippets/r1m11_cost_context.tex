The token cost reported above (\avgTokens{} tokens per SubmodelTarget, averaging roughly \$\avgCost{} per target and \$\totalCost{} across the \nTargets{} instrumented targets at the API pricing in effect during these runs \citep{OpenAI_API_Pricing_2026}) is small in absolute terms, so the dominant cost of the workflow is modeler time rather than compute. That time differs by collaboration mode: batch curation requires the modeler to review, and often reject or repair, drafts that may carry unit, source-selection, or denominator errors, while interactive extraction front-loads modeler effort during extraction but leaves little to revise afterwards. We do not report a controlled time comparison against fully manual extraction because the two were not run on the same targets under matched conditions, and net time efficiency depends on the model, the literature base, and the modeler's familiarity with the framework. MAPLE does not eliminate modeler effort; it records that effort in a form that can be audited and re-run, naming the decisions the modeler made and why, which a free-text extraction does not.
