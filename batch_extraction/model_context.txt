# QSPIO-PDAC: Quantitative Systems Pharmacology Model for Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma

## Overview
Immuno-oncology QSP model for PDAC capturing tumor-immune-stroma interactions and therapeutic interventions.

## Compartments
- **V_C**: Central compartment (blood, 5 L)
- **V_P**: Peripheral compartment (tissues, 60 L)
- **V_T**: Tumor compartment (dynamic volume, initially ~1 μL)
- **V_LN**: Tumor-draining lymph node (1113 mm³)
- Synaptic compartments for immune checkpoint interactions (syn_CD8_C1, syn_CD8_APC, syn_M_C)

## Cell Populations

### Tumor Cells
- **C1**: Viable cancer cells
- **C_x**: Dead cancer cells (source of antigen)
- **K**: Carrying capacity (vasculature-dependent)

### T Cells (by functional state)
- **nCD8, nTreg**: Naive T cells (in V_C, V_P, V_LN)
- **aCD8, aTreg, aTh**: Activated T cells (in V_LN)
- **CD8, Treg, Th**: Effector T cells (in V_T and circulation)
- **CD8_exh, Th_exh**: Exhausted T cells (in V_T) - reduced function, PD-1 high

### Myeloid Cells
- **APC, mAPC**: Antigen-presenting cells (naive vs mature)
- **MDSC**: Myeloid-derived suppressor cells (immunosuppressive)
- **Mac_M1**: M1 macrophages (pro-inflammatory, anti-tumor)
- **Mac_M2**: M2 macrophages (immunosuppressive, pro-tumor)

### Stromal Cells (PDAC-specific)
- **qPSC**: Quiescent pancreatic stellate cells (vitamin A storing, homeostatic)
- **aPSC**: Activated PSCs (myofibroblast-like, αSMA+, main CAF population in PDAC)
- **ECM**: Extracellular matrix (collagen, fibronectin - drives desmoplasia)

## Key Cytokines and Factors
- **Pro-inflammatory**: IFNγ, IL-12
- **Immunosuppressive**: TGFβ, IL-10, IL-6
- **T cell growth**: IL-2
- **Chemokines**: CCL2 (monocyte recruitment), CXCL12 (T cell exclusion)
- **Growth factors**: PDGF (PSC activation), c_vas (angiogenic)

## Checkpoint Molecules
- **PD-1/PD-L1/PD-L2**: T cell exhaustion axis
- **CTLA-4/CD80/CD86**: T cell activation control
- **CD28**: Co-stimulation

## Therapeutic Interventions
- **Checkpoint inhibitors**: aPD1 (nivolumab, pembrolizumab), aPDL1 (atezolizumab), aCTLA4 (ipilimumab, tremelimumab)
- **Chemotherapy**: nab-paclitaxel
- **Epigenetic**: entinostat (HDAC inhibitor)
- **Vaccine**: GVAX (GM-CSF secreting tumor cells)
- **Targeted**: cabozantinib (anti-angiogenic), anti-CD47
- **Immunomodulatory**: low-dose cyclophosphamide (Treg depletion)

## PDAC-Specific Biology
The model captures the unique PDAC tumor microenvironment characterized by:
1. **Desmoplastic stroma**: Dense ECM produced by aPSCs creates physical barrier
2. **T cell exclusion**: CXCL12 from aPSCs prevents T cell infiltration
3. **Immunosuppression**: High MDSC, M2 macrophages, Tregs
4. **PSC-cancer cell crosstalk**: Bidirectional signaling via TGFβ, PDGF, IL-6

## Key Parameter Naming Conventions
- `k_*`: Rate constants (e.g., k_C1_growth, k_CD8_death)
- `K_*`: Carrying capacities or Michaelis constants
- `n_*`: Hill coefficients
- `q_*`: Transport/trafficking rates
- `rho_*`: Density parameters
